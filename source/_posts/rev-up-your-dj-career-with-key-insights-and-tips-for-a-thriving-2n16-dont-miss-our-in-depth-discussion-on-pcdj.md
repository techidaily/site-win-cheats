In the video below from John Young of [DiscJockeyNews.com](http://www.discjockeynews.com) he provides 5 Tips for preparing your **Mobile DJ business** for more success in 2016\. Here’s his list, but be sure to view the video for a more detailed explanation of each **DJ Tip**:

1. _Change Your Packages For 2016_
2. _Change Your Marketing For 2016_
3. _Introduce Yourself To Venue Managers_
4. _Research Other Entertainment_
5. _Take Online Courses/Training_
